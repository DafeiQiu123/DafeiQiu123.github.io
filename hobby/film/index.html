🎬 Film / 电影

Film

I’ve loved films since childhood. Sitting on the sofa each week with family and friends, stepping into a story together, has long become a quiet ritual in my life. So far, I’ve watched more than 2,100 films. I don’t place much value on ranking “the best.” To me, movies are fragments of a life I carry with me—sometimes ropes I hold onto in difficult moments, sometimes landscapes I look back on in moments of joy. As part of this personal homepage, I’ve selected a set of representative works to reflect my taste and cinematic sensibilities. Rather than a list, this is a map of my own journey: from fairy tales to doubt, from admiring heroes to understanding ordinary people, from wanting to escape the world to learning how to stand within it.

我从小热爱电影，每周与家人和朋友围坐在沙发前，共同走进一段故事，早已成为我生活中不可或缺的仪式，至今阅片量已超过 2100 部。我并不执着于“最好电影”的排序，对我而言，电影更像是一段段被我带走的人生切片——有些是在低谷时紧握的绳索，有些是在欣喜时回望的风景。作为个人主页的一部分，我挑选了一些更具代表性的作品来呈现我的观影取向与审美方向，与其说这是一份片单，不如说它更像一张我走过的精神地图：从童话走向怀疑，从仰望英雄走向理解普通人，从渴望逃离世界，走向学会在世界中站稳。

Movies of the Year / 年度电影

“Movies of the Year” refers to the year I watched the films, not their original release dates. The list is unranked. I began systematically tracking and reviewing movies on Douban (Chinese IMDB) in 2019, which makes my earlier viewing history difficult to fully verify. As a result, this list is curated from 2020 onward as part of my personal viewing record.
这里的“年度电影”指的是我实际观影的年份，而非影片上映的时间，榜单不分先后。我从 2019 年开始在豆瓣上系统标注电影， 因此更早时期的观影记录与当年的“年度最佳”已难以完整考证。本列表从 2020年开始整理，作为我个人观影轨迹的一部分。
