Detailed Introduction / 详细介绍

EN. This project proposes a hybrid learning–signal-processing framework for generating professional DJ-style transitions between two songs. Instead of synthesizing raw audio (which is expensive and often unstable), we use a Transformer to predict time-varying DSP control parameters such as crossfade envelopes, multi-band EQ gains, HPF/LPF filter sweeps, and transition duration. A deterministic DSP renderer then applies these curves to the original audio to produce high-fidelity, beat-aligned mixes.

We encode the ending segment of song A and the beginning segment of song B with MERT as a high-level music encoder, and inject beat/tempo information through BPM-aware positional encoding to improve rhythmic alignment. The Transformer is trained with a masked-middle prediction setup to infer transition embeddings, which are decoded into structured DSP curves. This design keeps the system interpretable (mixing is described by familiar DSP knobs) and stable (audio is rendered deterministically rather than generated).

中文. 本项目提出一种“深度学习 + 信号处理”的自动混音方案，用于生成专业 DJ 风格的两首歌过渡。我们不直接生成原始音频 （成本高且不稳定），而是训练 Transformer 预测 随时间变化的 DSP 控制参数，例如 crossfade 包络、多段 EQ 增益、 高通/低通滤波扫频、过渡长度等。随后通过一个确定性的 DSP 渲染器将这些曲线应用到原始音频上， 从而生成高保真、节拍对齐的混音结果。

编码器端使用 MERT 提取 song A 尾段与 song B 头段的结构化音乐表示，并通过 BPM 感知的位置编码 显式注入节拍与速度信息，以增强节奏对齐能力。 Transformer 通过 masked-middle 预测生成过渡段表示，并解码为结构化 DSP 曲线。 该设计兼具 可解释性（输出即为直观的调音参数曲线）与 稳定性 （最终音频由 DSP 确定性渲染，而非黑盒生成）。
